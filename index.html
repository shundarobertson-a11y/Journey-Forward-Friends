Create a friendship profile

Browse & match for free

Who it's for

Different journeys. One friendship community.

♡

Cancer patients

Connect around friendship, everyday life, interests, and shared understanding without being reduced to a diagnosis.

✦

Cancer survivors

Meet others navigating life during and after cancer and build friendships for the road ahead.

🐾

Pet cancer community

A welcoming place for people whose beloved pets have or had cancer, including those remembering a pet they've lost.

Trust matters

A community designed around privacy, boundaries, and safety.

  • Platonic friendship — not a dating service
  • No detailed medical records needed
  • Block and report tools planned for launch
  • Clear community standards and moderation
  • Privacy-conscious profile controls
  • No selling personal health information
